# Eyes On Your Own Paper

You didn't study for the final exam. Move around the classroom, copy your classmates' answers and get back to your desk before the teacher looks up.

[Gameplay video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=O5RpgTyTd5w)

## Play loop

The game starts with a start screen that shows a short story and the instructions. When you press **Start exam**, the exam begins at **8:00 AM** and the clock moves one minute for every real second, so you have 60 seconds until **9:00 AM**.

### Goal

Copy **4 answers** from your classmates and get back to your desk before time runs out.

### How you progress

- **Find the answers:** walk around the classroom looking for the classmates with **light sparkles** over their desk. At the start of every game, 4 answers are given at random to 8 classmates, so they are never in the same place.
- **Copy:** stay next to a classmate with sparkles for **2 seconds**. While you copy, a bar shows "Copying answer… %" and you hear a pencil. If you walk away before it's done, the bar resets and you lose your progress on that answer.
- **Answer copied:** when the bar is full, a sound plays, the sparkles turn off and the counter goes up.
- **Back to your desk:** when you have all 4 answers, your desk turns green.

### How you lose

- **The teacher:** she is busy at her desk, but every 10 minutes of the exam a bell rings and, one second later, she looks up and sweeps the classroom from left to right with her **vision cone**. The cone turns red if she sees you. If you are away from your desk at that moment, you lose. If you are at your desk, you are safe.
- **The time:** if the clock reaches **9:00 AM** before you finish, you lose.

### After winning or losing

If you get back to your desk with the 4 answers, you win. In any ending, the game pauses, the music stops, a win or lose melody plays and a panel shows a message. The **Restart** button reloads the level and takes you back to the start screen, with the answers given to other classmates so you can try again.

**In short:** explore → copy → hide at your desk when the bell rings → repeat → get back to your desk to win, with the risk of getting caught or running out of time.
